Delete your Expense Pocket account

Expense Pocket · Last updated 10 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Expense Pocket and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. This cannot be undone.
What is deleted: your account, your email address, and all your wallets, transactions, budgets, categories and settings, on our servers and on the device.

If you cannot open the app

Email hovigtaslakian12@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Use the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

What stays

  • Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions.
  • Google may keep technical backups of deleted data for a limited time under its own policies.

See also the privacy policy.
