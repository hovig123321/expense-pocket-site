Privacy policy

Expense Pocket · Last updated 10 October 2026

Expense Pocket is a personal money tracker for Android, made by Hovig Taslakian, an individual developer based in Lebanon ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it.

In short. We store the money entries you type, so the app works and syncs to your account. We do not connect to your bank. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account and all its data inside the app at any time.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and sign-in details (email and password, or Google sign-in). Guest use creates an anonymous account ID.To create your account and sign you in.
Your age check: you enter month and year of birth once. We keep only a yes/no confirmation and its date, on your phone. The birth date itself is never saved or sent.To make sure you are old enough to use the app.
What you enter: wallets, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, notes, settings such as your primary currency and rates you type.To run the app and keep your data in your account and across your devices.
Crash reports and basic diagnostics (device model, Android version, app version, stack trace) via Firebase Crashlytics. Reports contain the error type and code, never your amounts, notes or names.To find and fix crashes.

The app currently shows no ads and takes no payments. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or bank logins. The app does not read your SMS or notifications from other apps.

Where your data is stored

Your entries are kept on your device so the app works offline, and in your account on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so it can sync. Data is encrypted in transit. Google Firebase processes data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

Who we share data with

Only our service provider, Google (Firebase), and only as needed to run the app. We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for third-party marketing. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all your wallets, transactions, budgets and categories are removed from our systems. Crash reports are kept for the period set by Firebase.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete account in the app, or see how to delete your account.
  • Access or correct your data: everything you entered is visible and editable in the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Notifications: budget alerts can be turned off in the app and in Android settings.

Children

Expense Pocket is for people aged 13 and over. The app asks your month and year of birth before creating an account and blocks anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use the security features of Google Firebase and Android, including an optional app lock (fingerprint, face or screen lock) that also hides balances in the recent-apps preview. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes to this policy

If we add ads or paid plans, we will update this policy first and tell you in the app. We will always change the date above.
