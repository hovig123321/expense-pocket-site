Free plan and ads

The free plan is free to use and shows ads: a banner on some screens, and a full-screen ad when the app starts and when you come back to it after time away. The free plan has limits on the number of wallets, budgets, bills and goals; the limits are shown in the app. Premium and Lifetime remove the ads and lift the limits.
